We returned home a week ago. We're always glad to be back, but we never really want to leave France. We got to see many of our friends here, but missed many, too, who were busy or gone the ten days we had in Bois-le-Roi. We'll be back in our "home-away-from-home" in France next April for our usual three-month stay, so we'll look forward very much to seeing all our great friends there then.  

Thanks for "tuning in" to our blog. See you in April! À bientôt!
