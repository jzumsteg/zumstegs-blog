<h2>Affolantes</h2>
Affolante is a name given to big ostentatious houses that appeared in this area along the Seine in the late 1800s. The railroad from Paris had reached Bois-le-Roi and so the rich folks of Paris built these as summer/week-end homes. We love to walk along the river and eyeball these places. Here are a couple of them:

The word "affolante" has several definitions, but the most appropriate for these houses is "staggering." For folks who lived in this area before the arrival of these mansions, "staggering" was a perfect description of these huge mansions.
